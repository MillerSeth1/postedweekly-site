Questions

Do I have to approve every post?

No. Every post is quality-checked before it goes live. If you ever want something changed or removed, reply to any of our emails and we'll take care of it.

Who writes the posts?

Posts are written with AI, using what you tell us about your business, and automatically checked for accuracy, specifics and tone before publishing. Anything unusual is flagged to a person.

What website platforms do you support?

WordPress sites connect in one click. If your site runs on something else, email us before you sign up and we'll tell you whether we can publish to it.

What if I don't reply to the weekly email?

That's fine. We'll pick a topic your customers are searching for and publish it on schedule.

Do you give medical, legal or tax advice in posts?

No. Posts for clinics, law offices and accounting firms stay general and educational, and include a short note that they aren't professional advice.

Who owns the posts?

You do. Everything we publish is yours to keep, even if you cancel.

Can I cancel?

Anytime. There are no contracts and no setup fee.

Will this get me to the top of Google?

We can't promise rankings, and nobody honest can. What steady, useful posts do is give search engines more of your pages to show and give visitors a reason to trust you.
